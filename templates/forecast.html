Mathematical Forecasting Methodology (Viva Reference)

This module applies a two-step Sales Velocity + Linear Trend Analysis model using NumPy:

1. Sales Velocity:
V = Total Sold / Active Days
2. NumPy Polyfit:
slope = np.polyfit(t, qty, 1)
3. Projected Demand:
Est = V × 30 × (1 ± slope)
4. Reorder Buffer:
Shortage = Est - Stock
Notice Products with 0 or 1 sale are appropriately flagged with Insufficient Data / Baseline Estimate, ensuring statistical honesty without dummy numbers.
